Your records go to one place: your own Google Drive.

Your phone or laptop
“Safri” sheet in your Drive
Safri’s servers: no vehicle records
What Safri can open
Only the “Safri” sheet it creates for you. Not your other files, photos or email: Google’s “drive.file” permission keeps it to that one sheet.
What Safri keeps
Your Google name, email and picture, and when you signed in. Nothing about your vehicles, fuel or services. Read the Privacy Policy
If you leave
Remove Safri’s access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The sheet stays in your Drive, yours to keep, open in Google Sheets, or delete.
No Google account?
Use Safri without signing in. Your records then stay in that browser only.
