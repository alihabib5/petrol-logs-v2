Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

What Safri does

Safri (formerly Sawari, and Petrol Log before that) helps you record fuel fill-ups, electric vehicle charging and vehicle services, and see your efficiency and costs. Your records are saved in a Google Sheet called "Safri" (named "Sawari" or "Petrol Log" in older versions) in your own Google Drive. We do not store your vehicle, fuel, charging or service records on our servers.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for: (1) permission to create and edit only the "Safri" sheet it creates (the drive.file permission), and (2) your name, email address and profile picture, used to show who is signed in and to keep the sign-in record described below. The app cannot see your other Drive files.

Sign-in records

When you sign in with Google, Safri records your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture link, plus the date you first and last signed in and how many times, so that we know who uses the app. This record is kept in a database hosted by Supabase and is only readable by us. Your vehicle, fuel, charging and service records are never sent there. Continuing without sign-in records nothing. To have your sign-in record deleted, email us at the address below.

What stays on your device

A copy of your records, your theme and currency choice, and your sign-in state are kept in your browser's storage so the app works quickly. Signing out removes the cached records from that device.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties. Your records are handled by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy; the sign-in record is stored by Supabase, our database provider, on our behalf.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the "Safri" sheet from your Drive whenever you like.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: aliartifical@gmail.com.
