Privacy

Petrol Log saves your fuel data in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. We do not store your fuel data on our servers.

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for permission to create and edit only the "Petrol Log" sheet it creates (the drive.file permission), and to read your name, email and profile picture to show who is signed in. It cannot see your other Drive files.

You can sign out at any time and remove the app's access from your Google Account permissions page. You can delete the sheet from your Drive whenever you like.
