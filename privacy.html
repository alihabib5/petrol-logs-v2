Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

What Petrol Log does

Petrol Log helps you record fuel fill-ups and see your fuel economy. Your records are saved in a Google Sheet called "Petrol Log" in your own Google Drive. We do not store your fuel records on our servers.

What we access

When you sign in with Google, the app asks for: (1) permission to create and edit only the "Petrol Log" sheet it creates (the drive.file permission), and (2) your name, email address and profile picture, used only to show who is signed in. The app cannot see your other Drive files.

What stays on your device

A copy of your records, your theme and currency choice, and your sign-in state are kept in your browser's storage so the app works quickly. Signing out removes the cached records from that device.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with third parties. Your data is handled by Google under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the "Petrol Log" sheet from your Drive whenever you like.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: add your contact email here.
